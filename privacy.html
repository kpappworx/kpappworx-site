Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 · KPAppWorx Technologies Private Limited

In short: when we run lead response or automation for a business, we handle its customers' details only on that business's behalf, we never sell them or use them to train AI models, our assistants say they are AI, and texts and calls go out only to people who have agreed to them.

Part A. AI automation services (lead response and workflow automation)

When a business (the "Client") hires KPAppWorx to run lead response or automation, we process personal information about the Client's own customers and enquirers ("Lead Data") on the Client's behalf. The Client decides why and how Lead Data is used, and we act as its service provider (processor). Part A applies to Lead Data. Part B applies to our website, Quarterdeck AI and our apps.

A1. What Lead Data we process

  • Name, phone number and email address.
  • What the person wrote in the Client's form or message, and when they sent it.
  • The content of text messages we send and receive for the Client.
  • Call audio, recordings, transcripts, summaries and outcomes (for example, appointment booked or handed to a person).
  • Appointment details and record identifiers in the Client's calendar and CRM.

We do not ask for, and we configure assistants not to collect, payment card numbers, government ID numbers or health information. If a particular deployment needs health information, we agree that in writing with the Client first and put additional safeguards in place.

A2. How we use Lead Data

Only to deliver the service to the Client: to send texts, place calls, book appointments, log records, produce the Client's reports, and fix or improve that Client's own setup. We may also use it to meet legal obligations. We do not sell Lead Data, use it for our own marketing, or use it to train or fine-tune AI models.

A3. AI disclosure

Our voice and text assistants tell people at the start that they are an automated AI assistant, and they say so whenever asked. Anyone can ask for a person, and the assistant then hands the conversation to the Client's team.

A4. Consent and opting out

We set up the Client's forms with clear wording so that people agree to receive automated texts and calls. We contact a person only after they have agreed or where the Client has another lawful basis. Every text offers STOP, and we honor opt-outs and requests not to be contacted across the Client's account. The Client remains responsible for having the right to contact its leads.

A5. Call recording

Calls may be recorded and transcribed so the Client can review them. The assistant tells people this at the start of the call. Clients are responsible for complying with call-recording laws in the places where they operate, and we build the notice into the assistant's script.

A6. Service providers (sub-processors)

We use providers to deliver the service, such as telephony and text messaging (Twilio), voice AI (Retell AI), workflow automation (n8n), AI language-model providers, and the Client's own CRM, calendar and email tools. These providers process Lead Data under contractual confidentiality and data-protection terms. We choose provider settings that do not use customer data to train models. We will tell Clients before adding a new category of provider, and we can provide the current list on request.

A7. Retention and deletion

By default, we keep call recordings and transcripts for up to 90 days. Summaries and records that we write to the Client's CRM or sheets stay under the Client's control. When the service ends, we delete Lead Data we hold within 30 days, after returning it to the Client if the Client asks, unless the law requires us to keep it. A Client can ask us for earlier deletion at any time.

A8. Security

We use separate credentials for each Client, encryption in transit, access limited to the people who need it, and secrets kept out of shared documents. No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting a Client's Lead Data, we tell the Client without undue delay.

A9. If you are a customer of one of our Clients

The business you contacted is responsible for your information. To ask for access, correction or deletion, contact that business. You can also email us at hello@kpappworx.com and we will pass your request to the right Client and help them respond. To stop texts, reply STOP.

Part B. Quarterdeck AI, our website and our apps

This Privacy Policy describes how KPAppWorx Technologies Private Limited ("KPAppWorx", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with Quarterdeck AI, our website, and our applications (together, the "Services"), and, in Part A, in connection with our AI automation services. By using the Services you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and contact information you provide — such as your name, work email, and company — when you request access, contact us, or create an account.
  • CRM data that you authorize us to read. When you connect HubSpot, we access pipeline and deal data through HubSpot's OAuth authorization, using read-only, least-privilege scopes. We do not write to your CRM.
  • Usage and device data — basic analytics such as pages viewed and features used, to operate and improve the Services.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Services — for example, detecting CRM changes, ranking deals for weekly review, showing supporting evidence, and generating pipeline summaries from verified facts.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Services.
  • To communicate with you about access, support, and product updates you have opted into.

3. Model training

We do not use your CRM data or other customer content to train, fine-tune, or improve machine-learning models — ours or any third party's. Where the Services use AI to generate scores or narratives, your data is processed to produce your output and is not retained for model training.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers ("sub-processors") who help us operate the Services — such as cloud hosting and AI processing providers — under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations, and only as needed to deliver the Services. We may also disclose information where required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain data only as long as needed to provide the Services or as required by law. You can revoke Quarterdeck's access to your CRM at any time through HubSpot, and you can request deletion of your data by emailing us. On request, we will delete or return your data within a reasonable period, subject to legal retention requirements.

6. Security

We use least-privilege access, encryption in transit, and access controls appropriate to the sensitivity of the data. No system is perfectly secure, but we design the Services to limit the data we hold and the access we request.

7. International transfers

We operate from Bengaluru, India, and may process data in other locations through our service providers. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers. Data residency options are available on request.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: hello@kpappworx.com
KPAppWorx Technologies Private Limited, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
