Trust and evaluation

Check it on your own history. Keep your data.

This page is for planning, security and procurement teams. It says what Udgass does with data, what safeguards exist, and what does not exist yet. Udgass is early software from one developer. It has no customers, no certifications and has not yet been run on a company’s real data.

At a glance

What exists today

What it isA local, read-only engine (command line, Python standard library only) that replays your past sales, compares its estimates with what happened, flags unusual weeks, compares stock options under assumptions you state, and keeps a tamper-evident record of decisions and runs.
Where it runsOn a computer you control. There is no hosted service and no account.
What it needsOne CSV with date, product and quantity. Nothing else.
What it does to systemsNothing. It reads the file you point it at. It has no connection to any ERP, planning or purchasing system and cannot place an order.
Who decidesA person. Outputs are advice with stated ranges. A named person makes and owns each decision.
Tested onInvented data only (94 automated tests). No measurement on real company data exists yet. That is what a pilot is for.

Evaluate it

The demand assessment, on your machine

You need Python 3.9 or newer and a sales export. Leave out customer names, emails and other personal data; columns that look personal are ignored and never stored.

Sales (required)Date, product code, quantity. Location if you have several. Daily or weekly rows; at least 39 weeks, ideally two years or more. Monthly totals are not enough. Sample file (synthetic).
Stock snapshots (optional)Date, product code, quantity on hand. Shows where stock-outs hid demand.
Purchase orders (optional)PO number, product code, order date, quantity, promised date, receipt date, vendor. Shows real lead times and whether they are changing.
Your past forecasts (optional)Product code, the first date of each forecast window, and the forecast total for that window, as it was made at the time. Enables a head-to-head comparison.
FormatsCSV in UTF-8, comma, semicolon, tab or pipe separated. Common date formats and decimal commas are handled; ambiguous ones stop and ask. Spreadsheet files must be saved as CSV.
  1. Check the fileShows the columns found, products, weeks, gaps and anything that looks personal. Changes nothing.
    python3 -m udgass check sales.csv
  2. Run the assessmentReplays your history, compares with simple methods and your own forecasts, and writes one report. A temporary working database is deleted when it finishes.
    python3 -m udgass assess --sales sales.csv --out assessment.html
  3. Share only if you want toAn anonymised copy replaces product and location codes. The report ends with a draft summary in relative figures only, which is never published without your written approval.
    python3 -m udgass assess --sales sales.csv --anonymise --out shareable.html

If Udgass is not clearly better than your current forecast or a simple average, the report says so. The view on whether a pilot is justified is advisory; the decision rests with your company. The first three assessments are free in exchange for permission to quote the result in wording you approve.

Data handling

What happens to your file

What the software does

  • Reads your file once, in memory, on your machine
  • Never modifies it (checked by an automated test)
  • Makes no network calls. Network and process modules are not imported (checked by a test)
  • No third-party dependencies
  • Writes only the report files you name and a small run log

Safeguards

  • Refuses files with extra columns that look personal (emails, phones, names, addresses), unless overridden. Best effort: it can miss free text
  • Run log records version, settings and file hashes, never data rows. Hash-chained, so edits are detected. Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof
  • Reports label which data they were measured on

What is missing

The honest checklist

Questions security and procurement teams usually ask, answered as they stand.

SOC 2, ISO 27001None. No audit has started.
Independent penetration testNone.
Customer referencesNone. No production deployments.
Real-data accuracy evidenceNone yet. Published figures come from invented data.
Integrations (SAP, Oracle, Snowflake, …)None. CSV exports only.
Hosted service, single sign-on, rolesNot applicable today. There is no hosted service to sign in to.
Sub-processorsNone for the engine, which runs on your machine. The website form uses Google.
Data residency and retentionData stays where you run it. Udgass keeps nothing beyond the files you create. If you cannot run it and data must be shared: written terms first, agreed fields only, encrypted storage, founder-only access, deletion within 30 days with written confirmation.
Support and uptime commitmentsNone. One person, replies within a few days.
Contract terms, liabilityNot drafted. Advisory use only. A pilot would be agreed in writing first.

  5. Named referencesOnly from customers who agree.
