What it measures

One evidence-based view of what your HubSpot stack is producing.

Quarterdeck sits beside HubSpot as an independent audit, optimization and reporting layer. It does not replace HubSpot.

Workflow ROI

Which workflows are healthy, redundant or silent. Outcome yield appears only when you import enrollments and outcomes, because HubSpot has no per-workflow outcome API.

Campaign & sequence ROI

Pipeline linked to campaigns by HubSpot's own attribution, kept apart from what cannot be attributed. Influence, not proof of cause.

AI ROI intelligence

AI-assisted actions detected in your workflows, plus usage you import. Never official billing, and never an invented credit count.

Lead quality & routing

Duplicates, orphaned records, ownership gaps and source performance, from metadata only. No names, emails or phone numbers are stored.

Action Center

Every finding with problem, evidence, impact, confidence, action, affected objects, priority and effort. Read-only: nothing is changed in HubSpot.

Executive report

A Revenue Efficiency & Confidence Report with methodology and limitations, exportable as PDF. Built only from computed numbers.

Four labels, no invented numbers. Measured: computed from your HubSpot records. Imported: reported by HubSpot or provided by you in a CSV. Estimated: uses assumptions you set and can see. Unavailable: HubSpot does not expose it, or access was not granted. Campaign metrics need Marketing Hub Professional or higher; anything your tier does not expose is shown as Unavailable.

The problem

The pipeline changed this week. Nobody has time to read all of it.

A CRM records every edit faithfully. It does not tell you which of those edits should change what you say in the forecast meeting.

Revenue leaders

Sales managers

Close dates move, stages slip backwards, owners change. Each edit looks reasonable on its own; the pattern across weeks is what matters.

RevOps

Building the weekly review by hand means exports, pivot tables, and a report that is stale by the time the meeting starts.

How it works

Connect once. Know what your stack is producing.

Quarterdeck reads your pipeline where it already lives. No exports, no data warehouse, no writing to your CRM.

01 · Connect

Connect HubSpot, read-only

Authorize Quarterdeck through HubSpot OAuth. Required scopes are read-only. Optional scopes unlock more modules and are requested separately.

02 · Baseline

Sync and build history

Deals, workflows, campaigns, sequences and records sync incrementally, with rate limits and permission gaps handled and shown. Snapshots separate one-off edits from repeated drift.

03 · Track

Compute deterministically

Fixed, tested rules produce every number. A language model may only phrase the narrative and cannot change a figure.

04 · Digest

Act on the evidence

Work the Action Center, drill into the records behind each finding, and share the executive report.

Questions

What teams ask before connecting a CRM.

Does Quarterdeck write anything to HubSpot?

No. Quarterdeck only sends read requests, and its code blocks anything else. One caveat: HubSpot has no read-only variant of its automation scope, which is optional and used only to read workflow definitions. Revoke it in HubSpot at any time.

Does AI decide which deals get flagged?

No. Ranking is deterministic: amount, stage, close-date proximity, repeated drift, staleness and material changes. Language models only help phrase the explanation — they never invent a fact or change a score.

Can it tell me what a workflow earned?

Not from HubSpot alone. HubSpot exposes no per-workflow outcome or revenue API, so Quarterdeck shows structure, influenced pipeline and associated opportunities, and outcome yield only when you import outcomes. It never states what a workflow generated.

Does it show my Breeze or AI credit spend?

No. HubSpot has no public API for credits or billing. You can import usage by CSV; it is labelled Imported and is not official billing.

Is this a forecasting tool?

No. Quarterdeck explains what changed and which deals deserve a look. It does not predict whether a deal will close, and it does not produce a number to replace your forecast.

Which CRMs are supported?

HubSpot. Nothing else is claimed as supported until it is built and tested against a real pipeline.

How long does setup take?

Connecting is one OAuth authorization. Quarterdeck then needs a short baseline period to build change history, because repeated drift can only be seen across several weeks of snapshots.

What happens in a quiet week?

You get a short brief. If little material changed, the list is small — a brief that pads itself to look busy is worse than no brief.

Can we disconnect it?

Yes. Revoke the connection in HubSpot at any time. Your CRM is unchanged because nothing was ever written to it.
