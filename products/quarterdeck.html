The problem

The pipeline changed this week. Nobody has time to read all of it.

A CRM records every edit faithfully. It does not tell you which of those edits should change what you say in the forecast meeting.

Revenue leaders

You commit to a number on Friday using a pipeline you last examined deal by deal weeks ago. The risky ones are in there somewhere.

Sales managers

Close dates move, stages slip backwards, owners change. Each edit looks reasonable on its own; the pattern across weeks is what matters.

RevOps

Building the weekly review by hand means exports, pivot tables, and a report that is stale by the time the meeting starts.

What Quarterdeck Does

Fewer deals. Better reasons.

Quarterdeck turns deterministic HubSpot changes into a short, ranked review queue. Every item answers why this deal, why now, and why before everything else.

Monday Brief

Start the week with the 5–10 deals that deserve review, ordered by materiality, timing, and the strength of the available evidence.

Deterministic Prioritization

Explainable ranking considers amount, stage, close-date proximity, repeated drift, staleness, and material changes. No opaque prediction model.

Change Intelligence

Detect close-date pushes, amount movement, stage regression, owner changes, stale late-stage deals, and past-due close dates from CRM history.

Repeated Drift Memory

See when a close date has moved repeatedly or a deal has returned to the attention list for several consecutive weeks.

Weekly Pipeline Digest

The same ranked evidence reaches your inbox before the forecast meeting: what changed, what needs attention, and what can safely wait.

Evidence-Backed Trust Context

Supporting context explains which slipped, stale, or past-due deals are making the current pipeline less defensible. It never pretends to predict the future.

How it works

Connect once. Know what changed every week.

Quarterdeck reads your pipeline where it already lives. No exports, no data warehouse, no writing to your CRM.

01 · Connect

Connect HubSpot, read-only

Authorize Quarterdeck through HubSpot OAuth. Access remains read-only and can be revoked without changing your CRM.

02 · Baseline

Build a change history

Quarterdeck preserves the deal snapshots and change events needed to distinguish a one-off edit from repeated drift.

03 · Track

Rank material exceptions

Deterministic rules evaluate recent changes, deal value, timing, stage, staleness, and repeated signals, then suppress low-impact noise.

04 · Digest

Review the brief

Open Quarterdeck or read the weekly email to see what changed, the deals that deserve attention, the evidence, and the recommended review.

Questions

What teams ask before connecting a CRM.

Does Quarterdeck write anything to HubSpot?

No. Access is read-only through HubSpot OAuth. No fields are updated, no workflows are triggered, and no automation fires while you review.

Does AI decide which deals get flagged?

No. Ranking is deterministic: amount, stage, close-date proximity, repeated drift, staleness and material changes. Language models only help phrase the explanation — they never invent a fact or change a score.

Is this a forecasting tool?

No. Quarterdeck explains what changed and which deals deserve a look. It does not predict whether a deal will close, and it does not produce a number to replace your forecast.

Which CRMs are supported?

HubSpot. Nothing else is claimed as supported until it is built and tested against a real pipeline.

How long does setup take?

Connecting is one OAuth authorization. Quarterdeck then needs a short baseline period to build change history, because repeated drift can only be seen across several weeks of snapshots.

What happens in a quiet week?

You get a short brief. If little material changed, the list is small — a brief that pads itself to look busy is worse than no brief.

Can we disconnect it?

Yes. Revoke the connection in HubSpot at any time. Your CRM is unchanged because nothing was ever written to it.
