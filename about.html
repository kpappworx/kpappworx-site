About KPAppWorx

The company behind Quarterdeck AI.

KPAppWorx Technologies Private Limited is an Indian software company incorporated in 2026 and headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Our flagship product is Quarterdeck AI, a read-only HubSpot attention system for weekly pipeline review.

Mission and vision

Mission

Make AI software businesses can actually run on.

Our mission is to close the gap between what AI can do in a demonstration and what it can be trusted to do inside a real business — reliably, securely, and without surprises. That means evaluation before excitement, and engineering discipline applied to every product we ship.

Vision

Earn a place in a real weekly workflow.

We're not building a portfolio of experiments. Our immediate focus is making Quarterdeck the fastest, most trusted answer to which HubSpot deals deserve attention before the weekly forecast meeting.

Company Story

From independent apps to enterprise AI software.

KPAppWorx was built on a simple habit: ship real software, support it, and learn from what breaks in production — then apply that discipline to harder problems.

2025

Origins

Before incorporating, our founder designed and shipped a series of independent applications on the Apple App Store — including Aiden AI, DeepFocus, and Gratitudes Jar — to learn what it takes to own software end-to-end: design, release, App Store review, and ongoing support.

2026

Incorporation

KPAppWorx Technologies Private Limited was incorporated in Bengaluru, India, to focus that experience on a harder category of problem: AI software that businesses depend on for revenue-critical decisions.

2026

Quarterdeck AI

Development began on Quarterdeck AI, our flagship product — a read-only HubSpot attention system that ranks the small number of deals worth reviewing each week and explains every conclusion with CRM evidence.

Why We Started KPAppWorx

Most AI never survives the gap between the demo and the deployment.

The prototype impresses everyone; the production system rarely arrives. That gap is never really about the model — it's about the engineering discipline around it: evaluation, guardrails, data handling, and the boring reliability that lets a business actually run on the software.

How we build

Evaluation before excitement. Read-only before write access, wherever a product touches a customer's system of record. Reliable infrastructure over fashionable infrastructure. Documentation treated as part of the product. Every system we ship is one we'd be willing to run ourselves.

Where we're going

Revenue attention is the first problem we're going deep on with Quarterdeck AI. The goal is to become useful enough that a RevOps leader actively looks for the brief every Monday, measured by time saved and review work reduced.
