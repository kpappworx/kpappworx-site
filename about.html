How we build

Rules we don't trade away.

Every one of these costs something — features, speed, or the easier demo. They are the reason the output can be defended in a meeting.

Deterministic first

Scores and rankings come from rules that can be read and argued with. A language model may phrase the explanation; it never decides the conclusion or invents a fact.

Evidence or silence

If the data doesn't support a claim, the product says so rather than estimating. A gap stated plainly is more useful than a number that looks confident.

Read-only where it matters

Anything touching a customer's system of record connects with the least privilege that does the job. No writes, no triggered workflows, no surprises during a review.

Narrow on purpose

One recurring question, answered properly, beats a dashboard of things nobody reads. Scope is what keeps a founder-led product trustworthy.

Shipped means supported

Ten apps are still on the App Store and still maintained. Releasing software is the start of the obligation, not the end of it.

Customers before code

New work starts with real customers. If nobody values the manual version, we don't build the automated one.

Work with us, or ask us anything.

Design-partner conversations, questions about how a product reaches a conclusion, or a security review before you connect anything — all welcome.
