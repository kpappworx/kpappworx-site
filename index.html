The gap

A CRM records every edit. It never tells you which ones to doubt.

What it catches

Six kinds of drift, each one shown with its receipts.

Quarterdeck keeps deal snapshots, so it can tell a one-off edit from a pattern. A short ranked list replaces the full-pipeline read.

Close-date pushes

A close date that keeps moving later is the most common way a deal stays in the forecast after it has stopped moving. Quarterdeck shows every move and the date it happened.

Amount movement

Material drops and rises in deal value, weighed against the stage the deal is in.

Stage regression

A deal that moves backwards is rarely mentioned in the meeting. Quarterdeck mentions it.

Stale late-stage deals

Late stage and quiet. Deals with no recorded activity while the close date stays put.

Past-due close dates

Open deals whose close date has already passed, still counted in the quarter.

Owner changes

A deal that changes hands mid-flight loses context. Quarterdeck flags the handoff.

Security and data

Built to pass a security review, not just a demo.

The same boundaries apply to every account, and they are enforced by what the connection is allowed to do, not by a promise.

What Quarterdeck does

  • Reads deal records and their change history through scoped HubSpot OAuth
  • Requests only the objects and properties its analysis needs
  • Ranks deals with deterministic rules and shows the evidence behind each one
  • Says so when the evidence is insufficient

What it cannot do

  • Update a field or write anything back to HubSpot
  • Trigger a workflow or fire any automation
  • Use your data to train models, yours or anyone else's
  • Keep working after you revoke access in HubSpot

Questions

What teams ask before connecting a CRM.

Does Quarterdeck write anything to HubSpot?

No. Access is read-only through HubSpot OAuth. No fields are updated, no workflows are triggered, and no automation fires while you review.

Does AI decide which deals get flagged?

No. Ranking is deterministic: amount, stage, close-date proximity, repeated drift, staleness and material changes. Language models only help phrase the explanation. They never invent a fact or change a score.

Is this a forecasting tool?

No. Quarterdeck explains what changed and which deals deserve a look. It does not predict whether a deal will close, and it does not produce a number to replace your forecast.

Which CRMs are supported?

HubSpot. Nothing else is claimed as supported until it has been built and tested against a real pipeline.

What does the free audit need?

A CSV export of your open deals from HubSpot. There is no connection to your CRM, so there is nothing to approve or revoke.

How long does team setup take?

Connecting is one OAuth authorization. Quarterdeck then needs a short baseline period to build change history, because repeated drift can only be seen across several weeks of snapshots.

Can we disconnect it?

Yes. Revoke the connection in HubSpot at any time. Your CRM is unchanged because nothing was ever written to it. You can also request deletion of synced CRM data.
