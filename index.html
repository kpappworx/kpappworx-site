Who We Are

An AI software company built for production, not demos.

KPAppWorx Technologies Private Limited is an AI software company focused on creating intelligent products that improve business operations, productivity, reporting, and decision-making.

We are an Indian software company, incorporated in 2026 and headquartered in Bengaluru. Our work starts from a simple premise: AI is only useful once it survives contact with a real business — its data, its edge cases, and the people who have to trust its output.

Why KPAppWorx

Engineering discipline, applied to AI.

The gap between an AI demo and a production system is engineering — evaluation, guardrails, data handling, and reliability. That gap is where we work.

AI-first engineering

Every product starts with a clear-eyed view of where AI genuinely helps, then we engineer around it with the same discipline as any production system.

Privacy focused

Customer data is never used to train models. Access is scoped to what a product needs, and nothing more.

Reliable software

We optimize for software that still works a year after launch — measured by durability in production, not by how a demo looks.

Modern UX

Interfaces built with the same attention as the systems behind them — fast, clear, and easy to trust at a glance.

Secure by design

Least-privilege access, encryption in transit, and clear data-handling practices from the first connection.

Built for scale
