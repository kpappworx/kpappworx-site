Company philosophy

Software that shows its work.

A standard, not a feature. Every product in the portfolio is held to the same four rules, whatever problem it solves and whoever it is for.

  1. 01

    Deterministic where it matters

    Rules and calculations that behave predictably when the problem requires a predictable answer. Same inputs, same result.

  2. 02

    Explainable by design

    You should be able to see how the software reached an important conclusion — and argue with it if you disagree.

  3. 03

    Evidence attached

    Important outputs point back to the underlying record, event or source. When the evidence isn't there, the product says so rather than guessing.

  4. 04

    AI with boundaries

    AI can summarise, communicate and make the interface easier to use. It does not manufacture evidence or silently change the underlying facts.

How we build products

From recurring problem to product.

Nothing becomes a KPAppWorx product because it would make a good demo. It has to pass through each of these steps, in order — and it is allowed to stop at any of them.

UnderstandSteps 1–3
  1. 01Find a recurring problem.Something a team deals with every week, not once.
  2. 02Work directly with the people experiencing it.Their words, their records, their current workaround.
  3. 03Solve it manually first, where necessary.If the manual result isn't valuable, software won't be either.

Gate: the manual result is worth having

BuildSteps 4–5
  1. 04Identify the repeatable logic.The part that is the same every time becomes the rules.
  2. 05Turn that logic into software.A deterministic core first; language and interface on top.

Gate: the software reproduces the manual answer

CommitSteps 6–7
  1. 06Validate it with real customers.In their data, on their schedule, with their feedback.
  2. 07Only then expand the product.Scope grows after usefulness is proven, not before.

Gate: customers keep using it
