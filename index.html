Why KPAppWorx

Engineering discipline, applied to AI.

The gap between an AI demo and a production system is engineering — evaluation, guardrails, data handling, and reliability. That gap is where we work.

Read-only by design

Quarterdeck never writes to your CRM. No fields updated, no workflows triggered, no automation firing unexpectedly during a review.

Every conclusion carries evidence

No unexplained scores. Each flag is tied to a specific missing activity, a stalled stage, or a dated record already in HubSpot.

A short list, not another dashboard

Five to ten deals worth reviewing this week — not fifty metrics to interpret before the meeting even starts.
