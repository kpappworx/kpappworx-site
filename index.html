On a job

Slow follow-up

How it works

Audit, build, then run.

You see the system working on your own business before you commit to the monthly service.

Step 1

Free lead-response audit

We look at how a lead reaches you and how long it takes to get a reply. You get a short written summary of where leads are being lost.

Step 2

Build and launch

We set up the system with your scripts, calendar and CRM, register your business phone number for texting, and test it end to end before it goes live.

Step 3

We run it

We monitor it, adjust the scripts and send a monthly report of leads, response times and bookings.

How we build it

Automation you can trust with your customers.

Your customers deal with this system on your behalf, so we build in safeguards from day one.

  1. 01

    It says it is an AI

    The assistant tells people it is an AI, and anyone who wants a person gets one.

  2. 02

    Consent built in

    We add clear wording to your forms so customers agree to be texted and called, and we follow US texting rules.
