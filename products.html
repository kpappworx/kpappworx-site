What Quarterdeck Does

Fewer deals. Better reasons.

Quarterdeck turns deterministic HubSpot changes into a short, ranked review queue. Every item answers why this deal, why now, and why before everything else.

Monday Brief

Start the week with the 5–10 deals that deserve review, ordered by materiality, timing, and the strength of the available evidence.

Deterministic Prioritization

Explainable ranking considers amount, stage, close-date proximity, repeated drift, staleness, and material changes. No opaque prediction model.

Change Intelligence

Detect close-date pushes, amount movement, stage regression, owner changes, stale late-stage deals, and past-due close dates from CRM history.

Repeated Drift Memory

See when a close date has moved repeatedly or a deal has returned to the attention list for several consecutive weeks.

Weekly Pipeline Digest

The same ranked evidence reaches your inbox before the forecast meeting: what changed, what needs attention, and what can safely wait.

Evidence-Backed Trust Context

Supporting context explains which slipped, stale, or past-due deals are making the current pipeline less defensible. It never pretends to predict the future.

How it works

Connect once. Know what changed every week.

Quarterdeck reads your pipeline where it already lives. No exports, no data warehouse, no writing to your CRM.

01 · Connect

Connect HubSpot, read-only

Authorize Quarterdeck through HubSpot OAuth. Access remains read-only and can be revoked without changing your CRM.

02 · Baseline

Build a change history

Quarterdeck preserves the deal snapshots and change events needed to distinguish a one-off edit from repeated drift.

03 · Track

Rank material exceptions

Deterministic rules evaluate recent changes, deal value, timing, stage, staleness, and repeated signals, then suppress low-impact noise.

04 · Digest

Review the brief

Open Quarterdeck or read the weekly email to see what changed, the deals that deserve attention, the evidence, and the recommended review.

Product Focus

Built to reduce review work, not replace your revenue stack.

Quarterdeck sits alongside HubSpot and your existing sales tools. Its job is intentionally narrow: decide what deserves attention before the meeting and show the evidence.

Quarterdeck does

Prioritize evidence-backed exceptions

  • Reads HubSpot changes and ranks only the material deals that deserve review.
  • Shows the dates, values, stages, activity signals, and repeated changes behind every conclusion.

Quarterdeck does not

Become another system to operate

  • It does not forecast with an opaque AI model, record calls, coach reps, or replace HubSpot.
  • It never edits CRM records or manufactures activity, probabilities, revenue values, or recommendations.

Security & Data

Built to pass a security review, not just a demo.

Access

Read-only and revocable

  • Connected through HubSpot OAuth — scoped permissions, revoke access in one click.
  • Read-only by design: Quarterdeck observes and prioritizes, and never writes back to your CRM.
  • Least-privilege: Quarterdeck requests only the CRM objects and properties needed for its analysis.

Data handling

Your data stays yours

  • Customer data is never used to train models — yours or anyone else's.
  • You can disconnect HubSpot and request deletion of synced CRM data.
  • Every surfaced deal traces back to specific CRM evidence. When evidence is insufficient, Quarterdeck says so.
