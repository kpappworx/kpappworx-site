Features

The audit trail underneath the swing.

Weekly What-Changed Digest

Every week, a plain-language summary of what actually moved in your pipeline — deals that slipped, amounts revised, stages jumped, close dates quietly rolled. Delivered by email or Slack before your forecast call.

Forecast Confidence Score

A 0–100 score for how defensible the forecast actually is, weighted by data freshness, activity signals, and stage history — not rep optimism.

Deal Risk Flags

Surfaces stale, unowned, single-threaded, and overdue pipeline automatically — the deals that look fine in a list view and aren't.

Revenue Scenarios

Best, expected, and worst case modelled from your own pipeline, so the range you present is grounded in deal-level evidence rather than a gut adjustment.

Stage Breakdown

See exactly where value is concentrated and where it's stuck — stage-by-stage, with the movement between them made visible.

Executive Insights

AI-generated priorities written as actions, not observations — what to chase this week, ordered by revenue impact, with the underlying deals attached.

Preparing for a board meeting? The same underlying data rolls up into a Board Readiness view and a first-draft narrative — see below.

How it works

Connected in two minutes. Answers every week after that.

Quarterdeck reads your pipeline where it already lives. No exports, no data warehouse, no writing to your CRM.

01 · Connect

Connect HubSpot, read-only

Authorize Quarterdeck through HubSpot OAuth in about two minutes. Access is read-only and revocable in one click.

02 · Baseline

Quarterdeck records the starting state

Every deal in the forecast is scored on data freshness, activity signals, and stage history — the reference point everything is measured against.

03 · Track

Changes get captured as they happen

Slipped close dates, revised amounts, stage jumps, and ownership changes are logged the moment they occur, so the movement isn't lost.

04 · Digest

The weekly summary lands before your call

A plain-language digest of what changed and what it did to the number, with the affected deals attached — by email or Slack.

Security & Data

Built to pass a security review, not just a demo.

Access

Read-only and revocable

  • Connected through HubSpot OAuth — scoped permissions, revoke access in one click.
  • Read-only by design: Quarterdeck observes and scores, and never writes back to your CRM.
  • Least-privilege: Quarterdeck requests only the fields it needs to score your pipeline.

Data handling

Your data stays yours

  • Customer data is never used to train models — yours or anyone else's.
  • Data deletion and residency available on request, with a clear record of what we store.
  • Every score traces back to specific deals and specific fixes — auditable, not a black box.
