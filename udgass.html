The gap

Organisations record everything. Few learn from it.

Planning, procurement, pricing, supply chain and finance teams make consequential decisions every day. The inputs sit in one system, the forecast in another, the decision in an email, and the outcome in a report months later.

Reports describe individual moments. Forecasts estimate the future. Neither preserves the link between the evidence available, the decision taken, and the result that followed.

Without that link, an organisation cannot tell whether its judgement, or its models, are improving.

Udgass is being built to maintain it.

How it works

From business history to measured judgement.

Five stages in a closed loop. Each builds on the evidence of the last, and the final stage feeds the first.

01

Remember

Establish a structured, time-ordered record of business events, forecasts, decisions and their outcomes, with the source of every input retained.

02

Detect

Surface structural change, recurring patterns and anomalies, and separate genuine signal from normal variation.

03

Predict

Produce probabilistic estimates grounded in comparable historical situations. Every estimate carries an explicit uncertainty range.

04

Evaluate

Compare scenarios and trade-offs against stated assumptions, constraints and risk tolerance.

05

Learn

Reconcile predictions with realised outcomes. Measure accuracy over time, expose failures, and define where the system has earned reliance.

Every conclusion is bounded by the evidence behind it, and says so.

The decision record

Every decision, with its evidence and its outcome.

The core of Udgass is a durable record that links an estimate to the decision it informed and to what happened next. That record is what makes performance measurable and accountability clear.

First application · Demand and inventory

Begin where outcomes can be measured.

Demand and inventory planning is a recurring decision with quantifiable outcomes and material operating consequences. That makes it a rigorous place to establish whether the approach works.

  • 01What range of demand should the plan prepare for?
  • 02Which products carry elevated shortage risk?
  • 03Where is excess inventory likely to accumulate?
  • 04How would supplier delays change the outcome?

Udgass is being designed to evaluate these questions against historical evidence, then compare each estimate with what actually happens.

The architecture is intended to extend to other planning and operational decisions. That extension depends on first demonstrating results here.

Designed for decisions, not for reporting.

Historical context

Read current conditions against comparable past situations, not in isolation.

Evidence-backed estimates

Each estimate shows its supporting history, assumptions, uncertainty and limitations.

Scenario evaluation

Compare outcomes and trade-offs before committing to a course of action.

Measured performance

Accuracy is judged against realised outcomes over time, not asserted.

Decision support, by design

Udgass is being designed to read from source systems and advise. It does not execute actions. Accountability stays with the people who decide.

Security and data handling

Designed to read, never to change.

These are the design principles Udgass is being built around. They describe intent. They are not claims about a deployed system, and nothing here has been independently audited or certified.

Read-only by design

Udgass is intended to read from source data and advise. It is not designed to write back to your systems or execute actions, and it will not need credentials that allow it to.

Exports first

Early work is planned around data exports your team chooses to provide, such as sales, stock and order files. That keeps any direct connection to your live systems out of the first stage.

Only the data it needs

The first application needs operational history, not personal customer data. We intend to ask only for the fields the demand and inventory work requires.

Traceable by design

The decision record is intended to show what data an estimate was built from, so that every output can be traced back to its inputs.

Agreed in writing

How data is stored, who can access it and how it is returned or removed will be defined and agreed in writing with each design partner before any data is shared.

  • Security certificationsNone
  • Independent security auditNot yet carried out
  • Data retention and removal processNot yet defined. It will be agreed with each design partner before data is shared.
  • In the meantimePlease do not send sensitive or personal data. Early conversations can use sample or agreed export data.

Principles and status

Reliance has to be earned.

Enterprise decisions need more than a persuasive prediction.

They need traceable evidence, explicit uncertainty, measured performance and clear accountability.

Udgass is being developed around those four requirements, and will report its own accuracy, including where it is wrong.
