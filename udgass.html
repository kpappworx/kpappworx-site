What would you decide differently if your business remembered everything?

Every sale, every order, every rush and every quiet week is already in your records. Most of it sits in spreadsheets and different tools, and nobody has time to piece it together.

Udgass is being built to read that history, notice what has changed, say what is likely to happen next, and check afterwards whether it was right.

We are starting with one question: how much stock to buy.

Ask it a plain question. Get a plain answer.

  1. STEP 1Exports first

    Connect

    Udgass reads the data you already have, such as sales, stock and orders. It only looks. It never changes anything. We would start from a spreadsheet export, so there is no technical setup.

    • Read-only
    • Starts with a spreadsheet
  2. STEP 2First focus

    Remember

    It keeps a clear timeline of your business: what sold, what you ordered, what it predicted, what you decided, and what happened after.

    • History
    • Your decisions
    • Results
  3. STEP 3Direction

    Detect

    It spots when something is different from usual, like a sudden jump in orders or a supplier getting slower, and tries to tell a real change from normal ups and downs.

    • Real change
    • Not noise
  4. STEP 4Direction

    Diagnose

    When something changes, it looks for likely reasons and checks them against your records. It keeps what it knows separate from what it is only guessing.

    • What we know
    • What we guess
    • What’s missing
  5. STEP 5First focus

    Predict

    It says what is likely to happen next as a range, for example “between 80 and 120”, instead of one made-up exact number.

    • A range, not a point
    • What-if options
  6. STEP 6Direction

    Recommend

    It suggests what to do and shows why, including what it assumed. You decide. Udgass never acts on its own.

    • Reasons shown
    • Assumptions shown
    • You decide
  7. STEP 7First focus

    Verify

    Later, it compares what it said would happen with what did happen, and tells you plainly how close it was. If it can’t tell whether your action made the difference, it says so.

    • Was it right?
    • Honest about doubt
  8. STEP 8First focus

    Learn

    It uses those results to get better for your business over time, and shows you whether it really is improving.

    • Gets better
    • Shows its record

How it could go for a small shop.

Meera sells handmade lamps online. This is an imagined story with made-up numbers. Udgass can’t do this yet.

Before

She guesses, every October

Two years ago Meera ran out of lamps in week two of the festival season. Last year she over-ordered and still held 300 in January. Each time she went on a feeling.

With Udgass

She sees what her history says

Sales climbed in the three weeks before the festival, both years. Her supplier took five weeks, not three. Last year’s sale made one week look bigger than normal. Udgass suggests 400 to 550 lamps, and shows how it got there.

After

It checks how it did

Meera orders 450 and tells Udgass. In January it sets what it predicted beside what sold, says plainly if it was close, and starts next year from there. She made the call.

What we promise.

  • It only reads your data. It never changes anything.
  • You make the decision. Udgass advises.
  • It shows its doubt. A range, never false precision.
  • It checks itself. And shares results only when they are real.

Questions people ask first

Can I use Udgass today?

Not yet. Udgass is still being built, and there is nothing to sign in to. The charts on this page use made-up numbers to show the idea. The early-access list is how we will keep you posted.

What is Udgass, in plain words?

Think of it as a memory and a second opinion for your business. It reads your past records, notices when something changes, works out likely reasons, says what is likely to happen next, and suggests what to do. Later it checks whether it was right. We are starting with how much stock to buy.

Will it make decisions or act for us?

No. It only gives advice, and it only reads your data. It shows its reasons and how unsure it is, and you decide.

What would it need from me?

To start, your past sales and stock numbers, for example a spreadsheet export. Joining the list doesn’t mean sharing any data.

Is it only for small shops, or only for forecasting?

No to both. We start with stock planning because those choices repeat and you can see whether they worked. Udgass is meant for planners, supply-chain teams and owners, and for single departments in bigger companies that want to try it on one problem. Over time we want it to help with other repeating choices too, like pricing and cash flow. None of that is built yet.

How will we know it works?

We will compare what it predicted with what happened, and with simple methods like a spreadsheet average. We will share results only when we have real ones.
