7. Method and limits

  • Estimates come from six standard methods; for each product the one with the lowest error on that product's own earlier history is used, with a preference for an equal-weight combination.
  • Each estimate used only weeks before its date (rolling origins). No future data enters any estimate.
  • Prices, promotions, calendar effects and stock-outs are not modelled as inputs.
  • Weeks with zero stock make recorded sales lower than real demand, for every method alike.
  • Past accuracy does not guarantee future accuracy.
