Questions owners ask first.

Anything else, write to hello@kpappworx.com and we will reply within one business day.

Will this replace my staff?

No. It handles the first minute: replying, asking two questions and booking a time. Your people still do the estimate, the consultation and the relationship. Anyone who asks for a person is handed to your team.

Do people know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. The assistant says at the start of every call and text that it is an automated assistant, and it says so again whenever it is asked.

Is it legal to text and call new leads this way?

It can be, when people have agreed to it. We add clear consent wording next to your form's submit button, include a STOP option in every text, and honor opt-outs. Rules differ by state and industry, and this is not legal advice, so have your own lawyer review the consent wording.

What tools does it work with?

It connects to web forms, calendars and CRMs that offer a standard integration or an API. Tell us what you use during the audit and we will confirm what is possible before you pay anything.

How long does setup take?

Most of the wait is carrier registration for business texting, which we do not control and which can take days to weeks. After the audit we give you a date based on your setup.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Running and monitoring the system, updates to scripts and workflows, a monthly report, and an agreed number of conversations. Extra conversations are billed at a stated rate. Phone numbers and carrier fees are passed through at cost.

Can I cancel?

Yes, with 30 days' written notice. The setup fee is not refundable once work has started, unless your agreement says otherwise.

Where are you based?

Bengaluru, India. We work with businesses in the United States, and most of our communication is written so that time zones do not slow anything down.
