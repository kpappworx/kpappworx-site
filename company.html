Structure

One company. Services and products.

KPAppWorx is the parent company. Its main business today is the AI automation service. Each software product under it has its own identity, its own customer and its own page — and carries the line “Built by KPAppWorx”.

Labs sits beside the portfolio, not inside it. It is where experiments and small utilities live until something earns a place as a product.

    How we work

    Small on purpose.

    KPAppWorx is founder-led today, and deliberately small. These are the habits we intend to keep as that changes.

    Small by design

    We would rather stay small and precise than grow a team before a product has earned it.

    Direct customer access

    Customers and design partners talk to the people building the product, not a support queue.

    Documentation as product

    If it isn't documented, it isn't finished. Internal systems get the same care as customer-facing ones.

    Evaluation before excitement

    New AI capability earns its way into a product only after it has been tested against real failure modes.
