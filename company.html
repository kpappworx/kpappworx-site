Culture

How we work.

KPAppWorx is founder-led today, and deliberately small. These are the habits we intend to keep as that changes.

Small by design

We'd rather stay small and precise than scale a team before the product has earned it.

Direct customer access

Design partners talk to the people building the product, not a support queue.

Documentation as product

If it isn't documented, it isn't finished. Internal systems get the same care as customer-facing ones.

Evaluation before excitement

New AI capability earns its way into a product only after it's tested against real failure modes.
